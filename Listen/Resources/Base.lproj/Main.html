Listen

Highlight anything on a web page to hear it read aloud, or ask about it.

Checking whether Listen is on in Safari… Listen is on in Safari. Listen is off in Safari.

  1. Open Safari Settings Use the button below. Listen closes while Safari opens.
  2. Turn on Listen In Extensions, tick the box next to Listen.
  3. Allow it on websites Highlight text on any page and click “ ”. If Safari asks, choose “Always Allow on Every Website”.
